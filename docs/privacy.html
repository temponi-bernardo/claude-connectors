Privacy policy

Updated on November 14, 2026.

This policy applies to the connectors of the Claude Connectors project, maintained by Bernardo Temponi, currently the Canvas PUC Minas, Gmail and WhatsApp connectors. They run only in the cloud and are used through the marketplace plugin or as a custom connector in claude.ai, Cowork, the mobile app and Claude Code. Canvas and Gmail run on the project's server; WhatsApp runs on a server that each person publishes in their own account (see the WhatsApp section).

What data is accessed in Canvas

Only the data of your own Canvas account, and only when you ask Claude for something: profile, courses, assignments, submissions, grades, announcements, modules, pages, files, discussion forums and calendar. The connector does not access any other system and does not see other people's data beyond what your account can already see in Canvas.

Gmail

  • What is accessed: the Gmail accounts you link yourself, with the gmail.modify scope, only when you ask Claude for something: search, threads, messages, labels and attachment text; and, if you ask, sending, replying, forwarding, drafts, labels, archiving and trash. The connector instructs Claude to show the recipients and content and ask for your confirmation before sending, moving to the trash or deleting a draft. The connector does not permanently delete email (that scope does not even allow it).
  • What is stored (in Cloudflare Workers KV), per person who uses the connector:
    • the linked Gmail accounts: email address, Google refresh token, granted scope, link date and whether access was revoked, in a single record encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored under a hash of the sign-in Google account identifier;
    • the authorization given to Claude: the sign-in Google account identifier and, encrypted, the sign-in email address and a session identifier. The tokens granted to Claude are stored only as a hash; access lasts 1 hour and renewal expires after 30 days without use;
    • while you link an account, a temporary encrypted request, deleted when you finish or within 15 minutes.
    The content of emails and attachments is not stored: it goes straight from Google to the conversation.
  • Isolation: each person only sees the accounts they linked.
  • Limited Use: the use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to answer what you ask Claude; it is not sold, not used for ads or model training and not read by people.

WhatsApp

  • Self-host: there is no project server for WhatsApp. Each person publishes the connector in their own Cloudflare account (a Worker and a Container) and pairs their own number. Nothing from your WhatsApp, not even the credentials, reaches the project maintainer.
  • What is accessed: chats, messages, contacts and media of the paired number, and sending text when you ask (one message at a time, with your confirmation and an hourly limit).
  • What is stored: the WhatsApp session and the message database stay on the Container's disk and, when it sleeps, become an encrypted backup (AES-256-GCM, with the SNAPSHOT_KEY that only you have) in the R2 bucket of your Cloudflare account.
  • Who gets in: Claude's access and the pairing page require a GitHub sign-in with an email address from the list you configured (ALLOWED_EMAILS); only the owner gets in.
  • How to delete: on the /pair page, unlink the device or start over from scratch; or delete the Worker and the R2 bucket in your Cloudflare account. On your phone, remove the device in WhatsApp → Linked devices.

Canvas credentials

  • The token you paste on the authorization page is checked against Canvas and then travels only encrypted (AES-256-GCM) inside the access tokens granted to Claude. The Canvas server has no database and stores neither the token nor the Canvas responses.
  • So that you do not have to reconnect every month, the connector extends the validity of the token itself in Canvas, at most once a day, when Claude renews access. No new token is created. If the token was deleted or expired in Canvas, the renewal is refused and Claude asks you to connect again.

Who the data is shared with

  • Canvas (Instructure / PUC Minas): receives the calls made on your behalf, as on the website.
  • Google (Gmail): receives the sign-in with your Google account and the calls made on your behalf (reading and, when you ask, sending and organizing the mailbox).
  • WhatsApp (Meta) and GitHub: with self-hosted WhatsApp, your server talks to WhatsApp as a linked device, and GitHub receives your owner sign-in.
  • Anthropic (Claude): receives the result of each tool you use in the conversation, under Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • The connector's hosting provider (currently Cloudflare Workers): processes the requests and may keep technical logs, such as IP address and time. The Gmail connector uses the IP address only to rate-limit requests, without storing it.

The data is not sold, not used for ads and not used for any other purpose.

This website

This website does not use cookies, trackers, audience analytics or scripts.

Actions on your behalf

Canvas: submitting an assignment and replying to a forum only exist with the submissions option turned on, and Claude asks for your confirmation before each submission. Gmail: see above.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Authorization follows OAuth 2.1 with PKCE and only returns access to allowed addresses, such as claude.ai and claude.com. The Canvas address is fixed on the server.

How to delete your data

Canvas: remove the connector (or the plugin) in Claude and delete the token in Canvas → Account → Settings. Without the token, the access kept by Claude stops working.

Gmail: ask Claude to unlink the account (the unlink_account tool), which deletes the stored token and revokes access at Google; when you unlink the last account, the whole record is deleted. You can also revoke at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
