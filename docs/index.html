Claude Connectors

MCP connectors that link Claude to everyday systems. The first one is Canvas PUC Minas: deadlines, assignments, grades, announcements and course materials right in the conversation.

Canvas PUC Minas

Ask Claude what is due this week, get an assignment's description with its rubric, check your grades or read a module's material, without opening the Canvas website.

  • Upcoming deadlines across all courses
  • Assignments with description, rubric and feedback
  • Grades by course and by assignment
  • Recent announcements
  • Modules, pages and files
  • Reading PDFs, slides and Word documents
  • Discussion forums and calendar
  • Search across all course content

Submitting a text assignment or replying to a forum only happens with the submissions option turned on, and Claude always asks for confirmation first.

Gmail (multiple accounts)

Search, read and manage email from more than one Gmail account in the same conversation, for example your personal one and your receipts one, including the text of PDF or Word attachments. Claude asks for confirmation before sending or deleting, and there is no permanent deletion: what goes to the trash can come back.

  • Search with Gmail syntax, in one account or in all of them
  • Threads and messages as plain text
  • Labels
  • Attachment text (PDF, Word, HTML and text)
  • Send, reply, reply all and forward with attachments
  • Drafts: create, edit, send and delete
  • Mark as read, archive, star, labels, trash and restore
  • Link and unlink accounts from the conversation itself

Install the gmail plugin and sign in with Google: that account is linked right away. For another account, ask Claude for a link to connect it.

Spotify (your own app)

Read and organize your whole Spotify library from the conversation: Liked Songs and playlists in full, with bulk cleanups that always show a preview first and save a backup before removing anything. Each person connects with their own Spotify app, so nobody shares a quota.

  • Search, artists with genres, history and top artists and tracks
  • Liked Songs, saved albums and followed artists
  • Playlists: create, rename, describe, add, remove, reorder and cover
  • Keep or remove likes by artist, genre, year or date; remove duplicates and unavailable tracks
  • Sort, split and merge playlists; find abandoned ones
  • Backups, JSON export and restore
  • Playback controls (Spotify Premium)

Install the spotify plugin and type /setup-spotify: Claude walks you through creating your app and connecting it.

How it works

  • Installation: in Claude, add the temponi-bernardo/claude-connectors marketplace and install the canvas-puc plugin. Nothing is installed on your computer: the connector runs in the cloud and works the same on the web, in Cowork, on mobile and in Claude Code.
  • Sign-in: the first time, you authorize by pasting a Canvas access token. The server has no database and does not store the token; it travels encrypted inside the access granted to Claude.

Each person only accesses their own Canvas account, with the permissions they already have. Details in the privacy policy.
